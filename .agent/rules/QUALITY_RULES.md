# QUALITY RULES

A lead is high quality when:
- company identity is verified;
- location/niche match;
- website status is reasonably verified;
- responsible is identified when possible;
- contact is appropriate and evidenced;
- opportunity rationale is grounded in observations;
- sources are recorded;
- uncertainty is explicit.

A lead is not high quality merely because:
- it has a phone;
- it has Instagram;
- it has no website;
- the agent found a person's name.
