# PROJECT RULES

1. Wake Prospector is an internal Wake Studio prospecting system.
2. Antigravity is the primary reasoning/operator layer.
3. Code is infrastructure, not a replacement for contextual research.
4. Never invent business, owner, role, phone, website, source or evidence.
5. Distinguish fact, inference, uncertainty and conflict.
6. V1 targets businesses without an identified own website.
7. Responsible identification is a first-class operation.
8. Phone association must be separately verified.
9. Prefer professional/business contact information.
10. Do not collect unnecessary private data.
11. Human sends WhatsApp messages in V1.
12. Interested prospects are handed to the human.
13. No artificial precision or fake confidence.
14. Preserve source information.
15. Preserve discarded leads and reasons.
16. Avoid unnecessary dependencies and infrastructure.
17. Do not build a SaaS in V1.
18. Do not add features merely because they are technically possible.
