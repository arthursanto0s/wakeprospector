# RESEARCH RULES

- Search broadly enough to avoid single-source bias.
- Verify entity identity before attributing information.
- Prefer primary/official sources.
- Use secondary sources for corroboration/discovery.
- Investigate missing information adaptively.
- Search responsible + company relationship explicitly.
- Search phone + responsible + company relationship explicitly.
- Treat social profiles as evidence, not automatic proof of ownership.
- Distinguish own website from directory/profile pages.
- Record uncertainty rather than forcing a conclusion.
- Stop when further research is unlikely to materially improve confidence.
