# SKILL — LEAD QUALIFICATION

Classes:

- **STRONG**:
  Multiple relevant commercial/digital signals + verified business in target location + confirmed absence of own website + verified usable WhatsApp/mobile contact + passed adversarial audit.

- **POSSIBLE**:
  Verified business without own website + useful commercial signals, but contact is a landline or has minor residual uncertainty.

- **UNCERTAIN**:
  Insufficient or conflicting evidence requiring further investigation.

- **LOW_PRIORITY**:
  Little evidence of digital/commercial activity or website utility.

Qualification must always be explainable in clear Portuguese prose with concrete evidence and adversarial audit notes.
