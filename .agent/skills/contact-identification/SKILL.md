# SKILL — CONTACT IDENTIFICATION

Goal:
Find the best professional WhatsApp or phone number associated with the selected responsible person or commercial/marketing decision-maker.

Validate explicitly:
1. `person ↔ company` (via QSA/CNPJ, CRMV/OAB/CRO, or official profile)
2. `company/person ↔ phone number` (via official Google Maps, Instagram `wa.me` link, Linktree, or official directory)
3. `phone uniqueness`: Confirm the phone is not assigned to any other business in the database.

Prefer:
- Verified 11-digit Brazilian mobile WhatsApp (`(DD) 9XXXX-XXXX` -> `55DD9XXXXXXXX`);
- Direct professional WhatsApp of the owner, technical manager, or marketing/contracting sector;
- Official clinic/company WhatsApp Business number.

Strict Prohibitions:
- NEVER invent, guess, or reuse digits from another clinic.
- NEVER add a '9' prefix to a 10-digit Brazilian landline (`(DD) [2-5]XXX-XXXX`).
- NEVER use the accountant's phone from a raw CNPJ record unless corroborated by the business's public channels.
