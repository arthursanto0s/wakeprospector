# SKILL — LEAD DISCOVERY

Purpose: Find real candidate businesses in the requested niche and location.

Do:
- use multiple search formulations across sub-regions, streets, and commercial centers of the target neighborhood;
- diversify sources (Google Maps listings, Instagram, Linktree, professional councils, local guides, CNPJ registries);
- normalize business names and check for duplicates by name, domain, and phone;
- preserve exact source URLs and access timestamps.

Do not:
- fabricate missing fields or synthetic phone numbers;
- assume a search snippet proves ownership without verification;
- stop at the first directory list without cross-checking.

Deliver candidate entities for `company-verification` and `adversarial-lead-audit`.
