# CONVERSATION FLOW

## First contact

Responsible known:

"Bom dia, [Nome]! Tudo bem? Falo com você, responsável pela [Empresa]?"

Responsible unknown:

"Bom dia! Tudo bem? Falo com o responsável pela [Empresa]?"

## If responsibility confirmed

Short introduction:

"Perfeito. Meu nome é [nome], sou da Wake Studio. A gente trabalha com criação de sites para empresas que querem fortalecer a presença digital e facilitar que clientes encontrem e conheçam o negócio."

Then ask whether they have interest in understanding how it could work for the business.

## If interest is explicit

Explain briefly and transition to meeting/conversation.

Do not promise guaranteed sales or results.

## Handoff

When there is meaningful interest, stop autonomous sales messaging.

Prepare:

- lead summary;
- what the prospect asked;
- objections/questions;
- relevant business context;
- recommended next step.

Then user assumes the conversation.

## Refusal

If not interested:
- acknowledge;
- mark NOT_INTERESTED;
- do not continue persuading.

## Not responsible

Ask, at most, for the appropriate contact when contextually appropriate.
