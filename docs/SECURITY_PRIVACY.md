# SECURITY AND PRIVACY

## Scope

The project handles business prospecting information and may encounter personal data.

## Principles

- minimize collection;
- collect only information useful to the prospecting objective;
- prefer professional/business contact information;
- record source and purpose;
- do not infer sensitive personal information;
- do not collect unnecessary residential data;
- do not expose credentials;
- do not store secrets in lead records.

## Human review

Personal contact identification must remain reviewable.

A contact should never be treated as verified solely because an automated search produced a matching name.

## Data lifecycle

Support eventual deletion/export of lead records.

## WhatsApp

V1 uses manual human sending.

Do not implement API workarounds or anti-blocking mechanisms.

The system must not be designed to evade platform restrictions.
