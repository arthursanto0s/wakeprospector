# DECISIONS

## D001 — Agent-first architecture
Antigravity performs contextual research and reasoning. Code provides infrastructure.

## D002 — V1 focuses on businesses without own websites
Businesses with confirmed websites are not primary targets in V1.

## D003 — Responsible identification is core
The system should try to identify the responsible person, not merely the company phone.

## D004 — Contact association matters
Name and phone must be separately evidenced.

## D005 — Human-controlled WhatsApp
No automatic sending in V1.

## D006 — No false precision
No artificial 0–100 lead score initially.

## D007 — Opportunity is not purchase prediction
The system evaluates evidence of potential website utility, not likelihood of purchase.

## D008 — Local persistence
Start with local data storage.

## D009 — Adaptive research
Research depth depends on evidence and unresolved questions.

## D010 — Handoff on interest
Meaningfully interested leads are handed to the user.
